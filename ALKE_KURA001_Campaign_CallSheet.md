## 7. Production workflow & tips

1. Generate each segment in Veo 3 **image-to-video**, seeded with the mapped photo.
2. For continuity, use the **last frame of each segment as the seed of the next**.
3. **Keep moves small & slow** on dial/case-back shots — less motion = less AI drift = more faithful.
4. Generate **clean (no-audio) plates**; lay VO + music + on-screen text in **DaVinci Resolve / After Effects** for frame-accurate, razor-sharp results.
5. Add text from the **supplied vector logo** — never AI-rendered type.
6. **Signature touch:** dissolve the physical engraved monogram (crown/case back) into the vector symbol on the end card to tie product → brand.
7. Keep the **same color tokens** across all three cuts so the campaign reads as one system.
